Lakeside city, a beautiful place to live. Especially good is an early morning lakeside walk. My favourite time to walk is on a crispy blue skied autumn morning with the sun casting longer shadows through the blazing glory of the seasonal foliage.

It wasn't always like this. My memories of childhood are of the struggle to scrape somewhere to live out of the rubble and devastation we found ourselves in after the Climate Wars.
